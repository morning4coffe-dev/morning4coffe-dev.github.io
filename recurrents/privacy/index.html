Privacy

Anonymous telemetry

Recurrents collects anonymous, aggregate telemetry to improve the user experience. Examples include how often a subscription is added or removed. This information is not personally identifiable.

Telemetry is used to understand product interactions and guide future development. It is not used for marketing or sales and is not shared with third parties.

Microsoft sign-in and OneDrive

When you sign in with a Microsoft account, Recurrents uploads app data exclusively to your personal OneDrive. The app requests access only to its app-specific folder, which OneDrive creates automatically.

No user data is sent to Morning4coffe servers. For Microsoft's data practices, review the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

Morning4coffe may update this policy when Recurrents or its data practices change. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated modification date.

Contact

Privacy questions can be submitted through Recurrents support on GitHub.
